# GenFragility-LLM 70B Confirmatory Run Hypotheses
[Pre-registration Timestamp: $(date -u +"%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ")]

1. On 70B models, Hub-targeted EPR_1 is significantly higher than Tail-targeted EPR_1, McNemar p < 0.01.
2. Hub Anchoring N=100 average EPR is at least 30% lower in relative value compared to Degree-Matched Random N=100, p < 0.05.
3. Hub pre-update logit margin is lower than Tail pre-update logit margin, Cohen's d > 0.3.
