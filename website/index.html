Popular knowledge is fragile—and contagious.

A factual update can damage knowledge several connections away. We show that highly connected facts are both more vulnerable and more likely to spread errors, then introduce PopAnchor to reduce the blast radius.

The measurement layer

FactProp turns factual structure into a measurable signal.

Each entity is a node and each verified fact is a directed edge. We define an entity’s structural popularity by how many verified facts point to it—its object in-degree.

  1. 01
    Ground

    Expand real-world facts, then verify candidates against Wikidata.

  2. 02
    Connect

    Link facts through shared subject and object entities.

  3. 03
    Measure

    Trace correct-to-wrong flips from one to five graph hops.

Popularity is structural, not a claim about cultural importance. The explorer reports forward-edge object in-degree and excludes inverse traversal edges.

From finding to intervention

Anchor a few popular facts. Protect many more.

PopAnchor adds a lightweight preservation objective during fine-tuning, keeping the updated model close to its original behavior on a small set of high-popularity prompts.
