\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Alias-based evaluation.} Our Flip Rate relies on
alias-normalized exact matching. Although we use Wikidata aliases to
mitigate formatting artifacts, semantically correct but lexically
distinct answers may fall outside our alias sets, potentially
inflating measured flips.

\paragraph{Graph topology.} \textsc{RippleEval} enforces an N-to-1
DAG to guarantee unambiguous downstream paths, excluding cyclic and
one-to-many relations (e.g., HasChild, mutual co-authorship)
prevalent in real-world graphs.

\paragraph{Single-edit setting.} We isolate one update at a time
with a fresh model. This cleanly attributes errors to a single
perturbation but does not capture interference between many
concurrent edits in continual or lifelong settings.

\paragraph{Update method.} Our analysis focuses on fine-tuning with
LoRA. Whether the connectivity-based account generalizes to
weight-editing methods (e.g., ROME, MEMIT) or retrieval-augmented
approaches remains open.
